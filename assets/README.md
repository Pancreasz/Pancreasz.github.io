## brain/neuropils.js

The 78 neuropil meshes used by the 3D fly brain (`js/flybrain-3d.js`).
Generated from `fafbseg/data/JFRC2NP.surf.fw.zip` in
[navis-org/fafbseg-py](https://github.com/navis-org/fafbseg-py): the JFRC2 neuropil
surfaces (Ito et al. 2014, *Neuron* 81:755) transformed into FlyWire (FAFB) space.
Vertices are quantised to int16; faces are unchanged.

Before publishing, check the data's licence terms and keep the credit line under the brain.

## noise/nocturne.js

12 s mono excerpt (22.05 kHz, 16-bit PCM as base64) of Chopin's Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2, recording
from Classicals.de, used by the Noise Cancellation demo. Rebuild with `node tools/build_noise_clip.mjs`
(headless Chrome decodes the MP3 in the site root). The composition is public domain; the recording
is Classicals.de's, so check their licence terms and keep the credit line on the page.

## noise/fpga-figure.js

The isometric drawing of the capture setup (DE0-CV board, breadboard, INMP441, CH343, jumpers) for the
Noise Cancellation page, generated by `node tools/build_fpga_figure.mjs` with the Anatomy kit
(`tools/anatomy-kit/`, MIT, from github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy). Don't edit it by hand: change the build
script and rebuild. `node tools/build_fpga_figure.mjs --audit` re-checks the geometry.

## painai/bangkok.webp

Static map of central Bangkok for the PAINAI section, stitched from 54 OpenStreetMap
tiles (zoom 14) by `tools/build_painai_map.py`. The live tile server refuses pages
opened from disk, so the site uses this image instead. Map data and image
© OpenStreetMap contributors: keep the credit shown on the map.
